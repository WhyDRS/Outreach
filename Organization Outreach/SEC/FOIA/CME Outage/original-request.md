To the Office of FOIA Services,
U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

I am submitting this Freedom of Information Act request on behalf of WhyDRS, a nonprofit public-interest association.

I respectfully request all records related to the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME Group) trading outage that began on the night of Thursday, November 27, 2025 and continued into the morning of Friday, November 28, 2025.

We would appreciate all SEC internal and external records concerning the outage, in any form or medium.

Specifically, please search for and provide all responsive records (including but not limited to):

- Internal email correspondence and other electronic communications (including attachments) among SEC employees or officials discussing the outage.
- Meeting agendas, minutes, notes, or transcripts from any SEC meetings or calls where the outage was addressed.
- Internal memoranda, situation reports, incident reports, analysis documents, presentations, or other staff reports prepared by SEC personnel concerning the outage.
- Records of communications (emails, letters, call logs, messages, etc.) between SEC staff or officials and external parties, including CME Group representatives, other regulators (e.g. the CFTC), self-regulatory organizations, trading firms, clearinghouses, or other market participants, that pertain to the outage.
- Logs or transcripts of phone calls, text messages, instant messages, or other communications involving SEC personnel regarding the outage.
- Any other documents or records (electronic or paper) related to the CME outage.

I authorize fees up to $250 to avoid delays.

I respectfully request a fee waiver. WhyDRS is a nonprofit entity focused on public education about market structure, investor protection, and systemic risk. The materials requested concern:

- A major operational event affecting a federally regulated financial market operator.
- Federal oversight of CME Group, clearing operations, and related systemic-risk management.
- Matters the public has a strong and documented interest in understanding.

The requested records concern direct and identifiable operations or activities of the Federal Government, including regulatory response, monitoring, and risk-assessment pertaining to a significant market-infrastructure failure. Disclosure will contribute substantially to public understanding, as I will publish all non-exempt materials and analysis openly for investors, journalists, researchers, and the general public.

This request is not being made for commercial purposes. WhyDRS does not sell data or research and receives no economic benefit from publication.
